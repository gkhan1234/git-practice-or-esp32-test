🌱 What is multiplication?

Multiplication is just a fast way of adding the same number again and again. If you plant 3 rows of flowers and each row has 4 flowers, you can add 4 + 4 + 4, or you can multiply 3 × 4. Use the garden below to see how it works!

📚 For Teachers & Parents

This simulator helps Grade 3 students understand multiplication concretely before memorising times tables. Key ideas to discuss:

  • Rows × Columns: The first number tells us how many groups (rows); the second tells us how many are in each group.
  • Repeated addition: Multiplication is a shortcut for adding equal groups (4 + 4 + 4 = 3 × 4).
  • Commutative property: 3 × 4 gives the same answer as 4 × 3 — the rectangle just turns sideways.
  • Connection to division: Sharing 12 plants into 3 equal groups is 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
