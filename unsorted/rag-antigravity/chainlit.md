### 🥽 Workbench Golden Rules

1. **Power Off First:** Always disconnect USB power or batteries before placing wires on your breadboard!
2. **Mind the 3.3V Limit:** The ESP32 is a 3.3V chip. Never feed 5V directly to its pins.
3. **Soldering Safety:** Soldering irons reach **300°C–400°C (750°F)**—hot enough to cook in seconds! Never touch the metal tip, always use the stand, and wear safety glasses.
